Program Overview

The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology develops future-ready IT professionals with skills in software development, networking, database management, and emerging technologies.

Students gain hands-on experience in building real-world applications and preparing for careers in modern technology-driven organizations.

Core Areas of Study

  • Software development
  • Database management
  • Networking fundamentals
  • Systems analysis
  • Application development
  • Emerging technologies
  • Information systems
  • Real-world project experience
